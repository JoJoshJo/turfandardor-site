Common Questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to the Me tab, tap Settings, then Delete Account. Your data is removed within 30 days.

How do I block or report someone?

On any user's profile, tap the three-dot menu in the top right. You can block or report from there.

How do I update my profile?

Go to the Me tab and tap Edit Profile. You can update photos, bio, fan team, and watching preferences anytime.

When does premium launch?

Turf & Ardor+ and Elite premium tiers launch June 11, 2026 — for World Cup kickoff. Sign up in-app for the founders' price waitlist.

I'm having trouble signing in.

Make sure you're using the email and password you signed up with. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in screen. Still stuck? Email hello@turfandardor.com.

Found a bug?

Email hello@turfandardor.com with a description of what happened. Screenshots help us investigate faster.

Reporting Safety Issues

If you encounter harassment, threats, or unsafe behavior, report it directly in the app and email us at hello@turfandardor.com. We take safety seriously and act quickly on reports.
